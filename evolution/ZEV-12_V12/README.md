# ZEV-12 / V12: Does information beyond the present help?

Evidence level: E2 / stage-report evidence.

## Stage question

V11 found no significant additional advantage from experience reuse in a bounded Markov environment. V12 moved to non-Markov conditions with delayed signals and delayed benefits to examine whether contextual memory could provide a strategic advantage.

## What the report records

The stage report records successful checks concerning delayed signals, delayed benefits, and sequence memory. Under its frozen configuration, repeated A/B comparisons showed a weak positive signal: a representative result was Δ≈+0.008, with positive differences for 7 of 10 seeds. This is a representative report result, not evidence of a stable effect across configurations or an independent reproduction in this review.

## What remains unproven

The report explicitly states that effect size and stability were insufficient to meet its “strategy dominance” threshold. It does not establish general memory ability, general intelligence, consciousness, production autonomy, or safety certification. No experiments were rerun and no statistical significance was recalculated from raw logs for this candidate.

## Why proceed to V13

The report records that increased environmental pressure did not materially improve results, while strict quality gating made triggering nearly silent. The proposed next direction was therefore to reconcile trigger frequency and quality through structural work rather than further parameter tuning. This is a proposed successor direction, not a claim that successor implementations were completed.

This release does not fill or validate the historical gaps for V3–V10.
