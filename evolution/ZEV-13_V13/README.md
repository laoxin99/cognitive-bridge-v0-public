# ZEV-13 / V13: How long does the information advantage last?

Evidence level: E2 / stage-summary evidence.

## Stage question

V12 recorded a weak positive signal from contextual memory under bounded non-Markov conditions. V13 asked whether information gained through local exploration could continue to influence choices over later steps.

## What the summary records

Under one bounded experimental setup, the V13 stage summary records that an advantage from locally gathered information remained observable in a window of about 20 steps. The observation applies to the reported experimental setup and does not establish the same behavior in the default runtime.

## What remains unproven

The 25-step window did not meet the same observation criterion in that setup. These are observation windows for one experiment; they establish neither a universal limit across environments nor an advantage at every intervening step. The underlying result file was not available for this draft, and no independent rerun was performed.

## Successor research question

The stage summary asks how locally acquired information can continue to influence decisions over a longer window. This question follows from the V13 negative result; this installment does not report successor results.
