# ZEV-01 / ZhiNao V1: Intelligence Begins With Reaction

**Evidence status: E1 / historical reconstruction**

This is the first chapter in the public reconstruction of the ZhiNao engineering evolution. It describes the research question that later generations developed from. It is not presented as a separately archived or independently validated software release.

## The question

Before memory, planning or language, a simpler question comes first:

> Can a system receive a stimulus and produce a bounded response under explicit conditions?

V1 frames intelligence at the reaction level:

```text
stimulus
-> bounded reaction
-> observable result
```

The important step was not complexity. It was moving the starting point of the project away from free-form generation and toward a reaction that could be constrained and inspected.

## What changed at V1

- a stimulus became an explicit input;
- a reaction became an explicit system output;
- the response was treated as bounded rather than arbitrary;
- the result could be observed by the surrounding experiment.

## What this stage establishes

V1 establishes the conceptual starting point for the later engineering line: intelligence can be investigated as a reaction problem before it is treated as a language or planning problem.

## What this stage does not establish

- learning;
- memory;
- internal state persistence;
- autonomous goals;
- consciousness;
- AGI;
- the capabilities of later private ZhiNao generations.

## Why V2 became necessary

A pure stimulus-response description cannot explain why the same external stimulus may lead to different reactions at different times. The next stage therefore asks whether an internal state must become part of the reaction conditions.

## Disclosure boundary

No private kernel code, state schema, scoring rule, threshold, runtime trace or later-generation implementation is included in this release.
