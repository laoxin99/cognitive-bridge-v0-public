## Evidence and interpretation

E1 denotes historical reconstruction, not an independently validated release. E2 denotes stage-report evidence, not an independent rerun performed for these publications. V13's cited raw result file was unavailable during preparation, so its public account remains E2. The observation windows do not establish universal limits or an advantage at every intervening step.

V3–V10 were skipped by owner decision in this publication sequence. The gap establishes neither completion nor independent validation. The linked stage documents remain the source for each statement and its limits.

## The question left open

How can information acquired locally continue to influence decisions over longer windows? This review presents that as the question left by V13 and includes no successor results.
